ℹ️ About the Project

Research & System Rationale

This web-based student help desk system is specifically developed for Senior High School students at Asian College of Technology – Bulacao Campus.

The system centralizes inquiry tracking, systematically monitors resolution progress, and provides an accessible channel between students and support personnel.
